## Kya banana hai (4 cheezein, isi order mein)

1. **Excel database**, `Media_Consumption_Index.xlsx`
   Rows: 6 countries (**India, US, UK, Japan, South Korea, Brazil**)
   Columns: 5 formats (streaming video, TV, gaming, live events, music/audio/short-video) × 4 metrics (penetration %, minutes/day, ₹ spend per capita/year, growth 2020→25)
   Plus: source column, date column, definition column (kyunki "time spent" ki definition har source mein alag hoti hai, yeh dikhana khud proof hai ki tum analyst ho), ek pivot, 3 charts.

2. **10-slide deck (PDF)**, 3 findings + har finding ka "so what". Topic titles nahi, sentence titles.

3. **1-page industry perspective (PDF/MD)**, publish on LinkedIn + Medium/Substack. JD mein "published research" likha hai; yeh usi ka proof hai.

4. **CV line + 90-second walkthrough** (niche diya hai).

---

## Deck ke 3 findings (yeh answer tum khud data se prove karna, shape yeh hoga)

1. **India is "time-rich, wallet-poor."** Consumption time global leaders ke barabar, per-hour monetisation uska ek fraction → yeh ek number India ke poore M&E ki economics explain karta hai.
2. **Growth time add nahi kar rahi, time reallocate kar rahi hai**, short-video/gaming wins, TV/linear loses. Isliye "naya audience" ka narrative mostly false hai.
3. **Live events + gaming are the only formats where both time AND spend are rising** in most markets → sabse bada under-covered pool.
4. *(bonus slide)* Apne earlier **profit-pool model** ka ek slide yahin daal do, "consumption kahan hai vs profit kahan hai". Do project nahi, ek hi deck mein depth.
